Test fixture written for Awlchemy (not a real product pattern).

Sizes
- Body: 40 x 80 mm, two layers glued and stitched.
- Panel A (front) and Panel B (back): 40 x 80 mm, 8 mm corner radius.
- Loop strap C: 20 x 47 mm before folding.
- Stitch line 3 mm in from the edge, 1 mm holes, 4 mm spacing.

What you need
- Panels A and B in veg-tan, 1.2-1.6 mm. Strap C in 1.0-1.2 mm, or skive its ends.
- One split ring 25-30 mm and one swivel clip.
- Waxed thread 0.6-0.8 mm and two harness needles.

Before you start
- Print this sheet at 100% (Actual size). Check the 20 mm test square with a ruler.

Steps
Step 1: Print the sheet and check the test square.
Step 2: Trace and cut panels A, B and strap C. Mark the stitch holes.
Step 3: Punch the stitch holes through each piece.
Step 4: Bevel and burnish the long edges of strap C.
Step 5: Fit the clip onto the ring, then fold strap C around the ring.
Step 6: Place the folded strap between panels A and B at the top edge.
Step 7: Glue panel A to panel B with a thin layer of cement and press flat.
Step 8: Saddle-stitch all around, backstitching the last two holes.
Step 9: Sand the edges flush, bevel, and burnish until smooth.
Step 10: Condition the leather with a little balm.

Tips
- Practise ten stitches on a folded scrap first.
- Keep glue away from the edge so it burnishes evenly.
- Use a mallet on the chisel, never a metal hammer.
